# Delegation Workflow

## Purpose

Assign bounded independent work without transferring ownership of the result.

## Inputs

- A separable objective and non-overlapping ownership boundary
- Constraints, evidence sources, verification, and stop conditions

## Procedure

1. Confirm that delegation saves time or context without splitting a tightly
   coupled decision.
2. Give the worker the objective, relevant context, exact scope, constraints,
   expected output, verification commands, and stop conditions.
3. State which files or responsibilities the worker owns.
4. Warn that other work may occur nearby and require preservation of concurrent
   edits.
5. Require the worker to report evidence, uncertainty, and any deviation.
6. Inspect the returned work and run the relevant checks as the main agent.
7. Accept, revise, or reject the result based on evidence.

## Stop conditions

Do not delegate architecture, subtle diagnosis, security-sensitive decisions,
or overlapping implementation when detecting a wrong result would be costly.
Stop a worker that escapes scope or lacks required authority.

## Output

A bounded result plus main-agent review and verification evidence.

## Optional accelerator

An external orchestration skill may schedule workers. Worker availability never
makes delegation mandatory.
