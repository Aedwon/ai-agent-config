# Design Workflow

## Purpose

Turn an uncertain feature or structural change into an approved design before
implementation.

## Inputs

- Current project state and governing decisions
- The problem, intended user, constraints, and success criteria

## Procedure

1. Inspect the relevant project context and existing boundaries.
2. Classify the change by consequence and structural reach.
3. Ask one decision-owning question at a time; discover factual answers from
   available evidence.
4. Offer two or three viable approaches with costs and a recommendation.
5. Describe boundaries, data flow, failure handling, and verification.
6. Remove speculative features that do not serve the stated outcome.
7. Obtain explicit approval for the design and record durable decisions.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the problem remains ambiguous, governing decisions conflict, or the
design requires authority outside the request.

## Output

An approved design with explicit scope, interfaces, risks, and success checks.
Approval of the design does not authorize implementation.

## Optional accelerator

An explicitly selected external design skill may conduct this workflow. It
cannot change precedence or mutation authority.
