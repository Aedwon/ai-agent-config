# Decision-Recording Workflow

## Purpose

Preserve consequential project choices without turning routine activity into
permanent ceremony.

## Inputs

- A decision that changes architecture, scope, safety, or future work
- Considered options and supporting evidence

## Procedure

1. Record the date, status, context, and decision.
2. List viable alternatives and why they were not chosen.
3. State consequences, risks, and any follow-up trigger.
4. Link evidence and affected specifications or plans.
5. Supersede earlier decisions explicitly instead of silently rewriting them.

## Stop conditions

Do not record guesses as decisions or claim approval that did not occur. Avoid
logging trivial implementation choices that have no durable consequence.

## Output

A concise, reviewable decision entry with provenance and supersession state.

## Optional accelerator

An external documentation skill may help format records. The project owns the
decision and its approval state.
